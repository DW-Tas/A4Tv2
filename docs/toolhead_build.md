# A4Tv2 Toolhead Assembly

This guide covers the basic assembly order for the A4Tv2 toolhead. The renders show a representative build; your hotend, extruder, carriage, ducts, and fastener lengths may differ depending on the parts selected.

Read through the complete guide before starting. Prepare the correct printed parts and hardware for your configuration, check that all parts fit without excessive force, and keep wires clear of fans, hot components, and screw paths throughout the build.

> [!NOTE]
> This is an initial assembly guide based on the current CAD. Verify fastener lengths against your selected components and do not use a screw that can bottom out or contact electronics.

## Assembly Steps

<!-- markdownlint-disable MD033 -->

| Step | Instructions | Reference |
| ---: | --- | --- |
| **0** | **Remove the built-in supports.** Remove the two highlighted supports from the underside of the main body. Clean away any remaining support material without damaging the surrounding slots. | ![Built-in supports to remove from the main body](images/build_steps/0-main_body_remove_support.png) |
| **1** | **Install and route the LED wiring.** Fit the LED carrier and route the wires through the main body as shown. Either illustrated route may be used depending on the available clearance. Keep the wires tucked against the body, away from the 2510 fan opening, hotend, and all screw holes. | ![LED wire routing below the hotend fan](images/build_steps/1a-LED_wire_path.png) ![Alternative high LED wire routing](images/build_steps/1b-LED_wire_path_high.png) |
| **2** | **Open the 2510 fan mounting holes.** Carefully run a 3 mm drill through the four mounting holes of the 2510 hotend fan. Drilling by hand is recommended so the fan frame is not damaged. Clear away all swarf before installation. | ![2510 fan mounting holes to open to 3 mm](images/build_steps/2-2510_3mm_drill.png) |
| **3** | **Install the 2510 hotend fan.** Place the fan in the main body in the orientation shown, with its cable following the route established in step 1. Install the two retaining screws and tighten them evenly. Do not overtighten or distort the fan frame. | ![2510 hotend fan retaining screws](images/build_steps/3-2510_duct_screws.png) |
| **4** | **Install the square nuts.** Insert M3 square nuts into each highlighted pocket in the main body. Make sure every nut sits flat and that its threaded hole lines up with the corresponding screw path. | ![M3 square nut locations in the main body](images/build_steps/4-main_body_install_square_nuts.png) |
| **5** | **Fit the 4015 blowers into the ducts.** Remove the front cover from each 4015 blower, then place the blower into its printed duct in the orientation shown. Refit the cover if required by your fan and duct combination. The fan should be a light friction fit; do not force it into the duct. Repeat for the opposite side. | ![Front cover removed from a 4015 blower](images/build_steps/5a-4015_remove_front.png) ![4015 blower installed in a printed duct](images/build_steps/5b-4015_install_into_duct.png) |
| **6** | **Attach both part-cooling ducts.** Locate the small tab on the duct and engage it with the matching slot in the main body. Slide the first duct upward until it is fully seated, then install its two upper retaining screws. Repeat the same tab, slide, and screw sequence for the duct on the other side. Check that both ducts sit flush and that no wiring is trapped between parts. | ![Locating tab on the part-cooling duct](images/build_steps/6a-duct_tab.png) ![Matching duct slot in the main body](images/build_steps/6b-duct_slot.png) ![Sliding the first duct upward into place](images/build_steps/6c-duct_slide_up.png) ![Retaining screws for the first duct](images/build_steps/6d-duct_screws.png) ![Sliding the second duct upward into place](images/build_steps/6e-duct_slide_up2.png) ![Retaining screws for the second duct](images/build_steps/6f-duct_screws2.png) |
| **7** | **Install the hotend.** Insert the hotend into the main body from below and align its mounting holes. Install the four hotend screws from above in the positions shown. Tighten them gradually in an alternating pattern so the hotend seats evenly. | ![Four hotend mounting screws](images/build_steps/7-hotend_screws.png) |
| **8** | **Install the PTFE tube.** Cut the PTFE tube squarely to the length required between the hotend and extruder, then insert it fully into the hotend filament path. The tube must not buckle or leave a gap when the extruder is fitted. | ![PTFE tube installed above the hotend](images/build_steps/8-ptfe.png) |
| **9** | **Install the extruder and adapter.** Place the correct extruder adapter for your configuration over the PTFE tube and align it with the main body. Set the extruder onto the adapter, confirm that the filament paths line up, and install the two mounting screws shown. Tighten evenly without crushing the printed parts. | ![Extruder adapter positioned on the main body](images/build_steps/9a-extruder_adapter.png) ![Extruder mounting screw locations](images/build_steps/9b-extruder_screws.png) |
| **10** | **Install the toolhead board.** Attach the toolhead board and its mount to the rear of the extruder assembly. Confirm that the board is supported at all mounting points and that screw heads or standoffs cannot contact solder joints. Connect and secure the wiring, leaving enough slack for service but keeping it clear of the belts and carriage mounting points. | ![Toolhead board installed behind the extruder](images/build_steps/10-toolhead_board.png) |
| **11** | **Prepare the carriage screws.** Install the two upper mounting screws in the carriage as shown, leaving their heads proud so the toolhead mounting slots can hook over them. Route the carriage cable between the belts and away from the screw heads. | ![Upper carriage screws prepared for mounting the toolhead](images/build_steps/11-carriage_prepare_screws.png) |
| **12** | **Mount and secure the top of the toolhead.** Align the mounting slots in the completed toolhead with the two prepared carriage screws, then slide the toolhead into place until it is fully seated.<br><br><blockquote>💡 <strong>Tip: Use a 2 mm hex driver</strong><br>Pass the driver through the long access holes in the toolhead to reach and tighten the two carriage screw heads.</blockquote><br>Support the toolhead while tightening both upper carriage screws. | ![Installing the toolhead onto the prepared carriage screws](images/build_steps/12a-carriage_install_toolhead.png) ![Upper toolhead-to-carriage screw locations](images/build_steps/12b-carriage_screws_upper.png) |
| **13** | **Secure the rear mounting point.** Install and tighten the highlighted rear fastener through the toolhead board mount into the carriage. Check from the side that the mount is seated flat and that no cable is trapped behind it. | ![Rear toolhead board mount screw](images/build_steps/13-carriage_screws_rear.png) |
| **14** | **Install the lower carriage screws.** Insert and tighten the two lower mounting screws in the positions shown.<br><br><blockquote>⚠️ <strong>Warning: Ball-end hex driver required</strong><br>The driver will enter each screw head at an angle, but the screw itself must enter the threads straight. Start each screw gently and confirm that it turns freely before tightening. If it binds or needs excessive force, stop, back it out, and realign it. Tightening a misaligned screw can strip the screw head or damage the threads.</blockquote><br>Check all carriage fasteners once more, then move the toolhead by hand through its full travel to confirm that the belts, wiring, ducts, and printed parts do not interfere. | ![Two lower toolhead-to-carriage screws](images/build_steps/14-carriage_screws_lower.png) |

## Final Checks

- Confirm that every fan spins freely and that its airflow direction is correct.
- Check that the hotend, extruder, ducts, toolhead board, and carriage fasteners are secure.
- Verify that no wire can touch the hotend, fan blades, belts, or moving carriage parts.
- Confirm that the PTFE path is straight and unobstructed by feeding filament through it by hand.
- Perform the printer's electrical, heater, fan, endstop, probe, and motion checks before heating the hotend or starting a print.
